# Product artwork

`Images/Waveshare-device-hero.png` is a presentation render made with the built-in image editing/generation tool. It is not a device photo or engineering CAD model. The exact interface evidence is in [the screen gallery](screenshots.md).

The hardware reference was the owner's photograph `IMG_3522.HEIC`, converted to PNG for the tool. Screen references were the current production-code renders `03-menu-coin.png`, `11-generating.png` and `12-mnemonic-wide.png`. Original source photos and their metadata are not included. Earlier background-removal photo composites and a rejected glossy render are not published.

Final prompt:

> Create an EXTREMELY PHOTOREALISTIC industrial-design product visualization, the quality of an Apple launch-page hero, NOT an illustration and NOT a shiny CGI watch. Image1 is the exact real hardware geometry/material reference, preserve its actual CNC enclosure proportions: flush black circular glass fills nearly the full face, very thin bright chamfer at glass edge, unpolished satin pale silver aluminum sidewall with extremely fine directional machining, softly rounded lower edge. The face must NOT have a thick shiny silver bezel or concentric chrome rings. No generic watch crown, no jewelry styling. Make three identical instances of this exact device, displayed almost straight-on at a subtle 8-degree overhead tilt that just reveals the lower satin metal sidewall, same perspective and EXACT same size. Soft broad studio area-light reflection, subtle material roughness/microtexture and convincing Fresnel glass edges, true OLED black, exceptionally detailed machining and soft specular gradients. Photographic natural material realism, no exaggerated highlights or fake scratches. Keep the glass unobscured and the screen art tack sharp. Images2/3/4 are exact UI reference artwork for left menu / center generating / right seed words. Map the artwork intact to screens; pixel typography and original muted green/grey/white palette, no redesign. Composition: wide transparent hero, three devices along a nearly horizontal line, left raised about 1% of diameter relative to center, right lowered 1%; overlap by ONLY 5% of each diameter, left in front, right behind. Visible silhouettes and perceived device sizes must match perfectly. All UI content stays visible. Refined, quiet, photographic product presentation with enough negative space around edges, no text or labels outside the devices, no ground plane, no added decorative elements. Prioritize making the metal, glass and enclosure look physically real and identical to the actual reference photo.
